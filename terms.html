4. Geographic Scope

The App's live automated coverage is currently focused on the Northern Illinois / Chicago metropolitan region, supporting:

  • Cook County, IL (Deeds, Mortgages, Liens, Court Lis Pendens, Assessor & Treasurer Tax Rolls)
  • DuPage County, IL (Conveyance records, Fair Cash Values, Property Lookup & Tax Installments)
  • Lake County, IL (GIS WABParcels, Assessor records, Treasurer Tax Portals)
  • Kane County, IL (PIN List MapServer, DevNet Wedge Tax Rolls & Sales)
  • Will County, IL & McHenry County, IL (Jurisdiction routing & Assessor records)

Results are not available or validated for properties outside this active coverage area. The App should not be used to assess title risk for properties in unsupported jurisdictions.
