    Help & Documentation

    How to Use TitleRiskScanner

    TitleRiskScanner enables real estate investors, attorneys, lenders, and title professionals to instantly analyze property title risk, recorded encumbrances, and municipal tax liabilities across the Chicago metropolitan area.

    1. Searching for a Property

    • Search by Address: Enter the street address and municipality (e.g. 69 W Washington St, Chicago or 421 N County Farm Rd, Wheaton).
    • Search by PIN: You can also search directly by 10-digit (Collar Counties) or 14-digit (Cook County) Property Index Number (e.g. 17-06-400-033-0000).
    • County Jurisdiction: By default, Auto-Detect County uses the US Census Bureau Geocoder to identify the county and route to the correct land records server. You can also explicitly select a county from the dropdown.

    2. Interpreting the Title Risk Score

    • RED (High Risk): Critical title clouds directly threatening property ownership, such as unreleased mortgages from prior owners, active tax liens against the current owner, active court judgments, or pending Lis Pendens foreclosure lawsuits.
    • ORANGE (Moderate Risk): Active mechanics / construction liens (< 2 years), grantor/grantee deed discrepancies, or unverified GIS parcel addresses requiring closing verification.
    • YELLOW (Advisory / Routine Notice): Standard property financing (active mortgages held by current owner in good standing, satisfied via closing escrow payoff), utility easements, restrictive covenants (CC&Rs), ancient mortgages (≥ 25 years), expired mechanic's liens (≥ 2 years), expired judgments (≥ 7 years), or historical court filings pre-dating deed conveyances.
    • GREEN (Clear / Safe): Clean recorded title history with no unreleased mortgages, no active liens, and clear chain of deed conveyances in the examined public records.

    Title Risk Decision Matrix (How Status Flags Are Assigned)

    TitleRiskScanner evaluates public land records using title-underwriting standards to differentiate active clouds that threaten ownership from routine financing and historical records:

    Category Recorded Situation / Conditions Status Flag Title Impact & Legal Basis Closing / Buyer Action
    Mortgage Active recorded mortgage where borrower matches current owner (< 25 yrs) YELLOW Standard Property Financing: Routine financing in good standing. Does not cloud title; over 60% of homes have active mortgages. Standard closing escrow obtains lender payoff letter and records Release Deed upon funding.
    Mortgage Unreleased mortgage recorded by a prior owner (< 25 yrs) RED Unreleased Prior Lien (Title Cloud): Prior lender retains an open recorded financial lien against the real estate. Seller or prior lender must provide verified payoff release or title indemnification prior to closing.
    Mortgage Unreleased mortgage recorded ≥ 25 years ago (or ≥ 15 yrs with subsequent deeds) YELLOW Ancient Mortgage (Presumed Satisfied): Lapsed under Illinois Ancient Mortgage Act (735 ILCS 5/13-116). Informational historical record. Waived / omitted by title insurance underwriter.
    Tax Lien Delinquent tax lien filed against current owner (< 10 yrs) RED Active Government Lien: IRS, State Department of Revenue, or County holds super-priority lien on the real estate. Seller must satisfy tax liability in full from closing proceeds and record Certificate of Discharge.
    Tax Lien Tax lien filed against prior owner pre-dating subsequent warranty deed transfer YELLOW Historical Tax Record: Pre-dates insured conveyance; extinguished or protected by existing owner's title policy. Routine archive record. Confirm standard title insurance policy covers past tax liens.
    Judgment Lien Court money judgment against current owner (< 7 yrs) RED Active Judgment Lien: Judgment creditor holds enforceable claim and standing for judicial execution against the real estate. Must obtain certified court satisfaction and recorded release prior to closing.
    Judgment Lien Money judgment ≥ 7 years old OR recorded against prior owner YELLOW Expired Judgment Lien: Judgment liens expire after 7 years under Illinois law (735 ILCS 5/12-108) unless revived. Informational historical record. Lapsed by statute of limitations; waived by title company.
    Mechanic's Lien Contractor / construction claim recorded < 2 years ago ORANGE Active Construction Lien: Open statutory claim for unpaid building repairs, improvements, or materials. Demand sworn contractor statements, final lien waivers from all trades, or escrow payoff.
    Mechanic's Lien Contractor lien recorded ≥ 2 years ago with no recorded foreclosure suit YELLOW Expired Mechanic's Lien: Enforcement rights expire after 2 years under 770 ILCS 60/9. Informational record. Closing title insurer will omit expired lien based on statutory lapse.
    Court Filing (Lis Pendens) Active court lawsuit filed < 7 years ago with no subsequent deed transfer RED Active Pending Lawsuit: Active court case pending that directly affects property ownership or foreclosure. Do NOT close while pending. Lawsuit must be resolved, dismissed with prejudice, and release recorded.
    Court Filing (Lis Pendens) Historical court filing (e.g. 1994) ≥ 7 years old OR followed by deed conveyances YELLOW Historical Court Record (Stale): Resolved historical docket; subsequent warranty deeds establish modern marketable title. Routine archive record. Title companies routinely waive ancient court filings where subsequent deeds exist.
    Easement Recorded utility or municipal infrastructure access right-of-way YELLOW Permitted Land Use: Standard and routine for most homes. Restricts permanent structures over easement area. Review land survey plat to ensure easement does not interfere with intended improvements or additions.
    Restriction Recorded subdivision covenants, conditions, and restrictions (CC&Rs) / HOA rules YELLOW Routine Neighborhood Covenants: Regulates architectural standards, leasing limits, and HOA dues. Review recorded CC&Rs to confirm compatibility with intended property usage.
    Chain of Title Grantee on latest recorded deed does not match current record owner ORANGE Deed Name Discrepancy: Possible gap in chain of title, unrecorded transfer, or probate/marital change. Require seller or title company to provide Affidavit of Title, death/marriage certificate, or corrective deed.
    GIS Parcel Verification Entered address could not be matched to an authoritative county GIS parcel boundary ORANGE Unverified Parcel Address: No recorded legal documents could be verified without confirmed PIN. Verify street spelling, municipality, or search using official 10-digit or 14-digit Property Index Number (PIN).
    Clean Title History All recorded mortgages released, no active liens, no pending court litigation GREEN Clear / Safe Title: Examined public land records show clean ownership history with no open clouds. Proceed with routine closing procedures and standard owner's title insurance policy.

    3. Navigating Investigation Tabs

    • Findings: Lists every flagged risk factor with the rule ID, risk category, and factual evidence found in county records.
    • Tax & Assessment: Displays current land, building, and total assessed values, property class, tax districts, and Cook County Treasurer billed amounts, paid installments, and outstanding balances.
    • Liens: Correlates recorded liens against subsequent recorded releases/satisfactions to surface open vs discharged claims.
    • Recorded Documents: Filter and sort all deeds, mortgages, releases, and legal instruments recorded on the property.

    Every scanned property is automatically saved to your private local database on this computer. You can reopen past investigations or remove entries from the portfolio table at any time.
