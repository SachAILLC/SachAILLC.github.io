Core Capabilities

Mortgage & Release Correlation

Intelligently correlates recorded release deeds, certificates of release, and payoff records against prior mortgages using instrument numbers and grantors/grantees to surface unreleased loans.

Live Property Tax & Assessments

Queries county treasurer and assessment portals to extract assessed land and building valuations, installment payment histories, and verify zero-balance status.

Liens & Court Filings

Screens for unreleased HOA liens, state/federal tax liens, mechanic's liens, and court lis pendens foreclosure filings in official county recording divisions.
